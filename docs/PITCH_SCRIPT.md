# ⏱️ EarthGuard AI — 2-Minute Winning Hackathon Pitch Script
**Theme**: AI for Space & Earth Intelligence  
**Duration**: 120 Seconds (2 Minutes Sharp)  
**Presenter(s)**: Team of 3 (or Single Pitch Lead with Teammate driving live UI)

---

## 🎬 Minute 0:00 - 0:25 // The Problem (The Hook)

> **[SPEAKER]**:  
> *"Judges, every single day, orbital constellations beam down terabytes of satellite imagery tracking our forests, coastlines, and disaster zones. But here is the catastrophic bottleneck:  
> **Detecting change is easy. Knowing what to DO about it, where people are in danger, and who to dispatch first... is agonizingly slow.**  
> 
> Right now, competing solutions hand field teams a flat, unranked heatmap with thousands of red pixels. An analyst has to squint and guess: Is this a critical dam breach threatening 5,000 residents, or just natural seasonal dry-off?  
> 
> We built **EarthGuard AI** — not just another change detection tool, but an **autonomous planetary triage system**."*

---

## 🚀 Minute 0:25 - 0:55 // Core System & Live Demo

> **[SPEAKER]**:  
> *(Teammate selects "Amazon Rainforest Deforestation" on the live UI)*  
> 
> *"EarthGuard AI ingests temporal satellite pairs — here we have Sentinel-2 observations over the Amazon.  
> With our classical CV and multi-band spectral pipeline, we instantly isolate surface anomalies in under 150 milliseconds.  
> 
> Watch this interactive **Split Curtain**: sliding across reveals the clear-cut fishbone corridors tearing through the pristine canopy.  
> 
> But we didn't stop at simple computer vision. We built three core novelties that transform raw imagery into instant field operations."*

---

## 💡 Minute 0:55 - 1:35 // The 3 Novel Differentiators

### 🥇 Novelty 1: Severity-Ranked Prioritization
> **[SPEAKER]**:  
> *"Look at our Tactical Response Queue on the right. Notice how the anomalies are **not a flat list**.  
> Every single detected zone is calculated through an explainable composite **Urgency Score**: balancing spectral change magnitude, contiguous hectare size, and crucially — **proximity to human settlements and critical infrastructure**.  
> 
> **Zone-01** immediately surfaces as **CRITICAL (Score: 68.8)** because the timber logging has breached the secondary perimeter of the Nova Esperança community. A routine clearing 20 kilometers away is de-prioritized to Low."*

### 🥈 Novelty 2: Confidence-Aware Transparency
> **[SPEAKER]**:  
> *(Teammate hovers over the 96% High Confidence badge)*  
> *"Other AI systems hide their hallucinations. EarthGuard AI surfaces uncertainty as a first-class metric.  
> We evaluate radiometric sensor noise and localized cloud-cover variance. If a zone is obscured by cloud haze, the system explicitly flags **'Needs Field Verification'** so commanders never send teams on wild goose chases."*

### 🥉 Novelty 3: Natural-Language Operational Briefs
> **[SPEAKER]**:  
> *(Teammate clicks 'Read Aloud' on Zone-01)*  
> *(Browser voice reads out: 'ZONE-01 exhibits a critical 32% spectral change signature... Operational Protocol: Dispatch environmental ranger enforcement unit within 48 hours.')*  
> 
> *"Instead of handing a commander a CSV of coordinates, EarthGuard synthesizes structured, decision-ready operational briefs — complete with recommended protocols and dispatch timelines."*

---

## 🌍 Minute 1:35 - 2:00 // Multi-Disaster Versatility & Closing

> **[SPEAKER]**:  
> *(Teammate switches dataset to 'Derna Flash Flood Inundation')*  
> *"Our spectral classifier works across multi-hazard domains: from Amazon Deforestation, to catastrophic Mediterranean Flash Flooding in Derna, to California Wildfires.  
> 
> With one click on **'Export Incident Docket'**, we produce an immediate, printable crisis docket for civil defense, FEMA, and UN relief agencies.  
> 
> **EarthGuard AI turns silent orbital pixels into lifesaving frontline action.** Thank you!"*
